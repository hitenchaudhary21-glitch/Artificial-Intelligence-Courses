## Parts Mapped to Skill Levels

| Part | Focus | Chapters | Estimated Time |
|------|-------|----------|----------------|
| Part 1 | Introduction to AI | Chapters 1–5 | 1–2 weeks |
| Part 2 | How AI Thinks | Chapters 6–9 | 1–2 weeks |

**What you will achieve:** A solid understanding of what AI is, how it evolved, how it processes information, and how it compares to human thinking. You will develop the conceptual vocabulary needed for everything that follows.

### 🌿 Intermediate Level (Parts 3–5)

| Part | Focus | Chapters | Estimated Time |
|------|-------|----------|----------------|

| Part 5 | Deep Learning | Chapters 19–22 | 2–3 weeks |

**What you will achieve:** Mathematical intuition for AI algorithms, hands-on experience with Machine Learning workflows, and a working understanding of Deep Learning architectures including neural networks, CNNs, and RNNs.

**What you will achieve:** Deep knowledge of modern AI systems including LLMs and Generative AI, the ability to architect and deploy production AI systems, and a clear career strategy with portfolio projects.

| Section | Focus | Chapters | Estimated Time |

## Time Estimates

The total estimated time to complete the book is **17–20 weeks** at a pace of approximately **5–7 hours per week**. These estimates assume you are completing the reading, teaching exercises, and practice sections for each chapter.

| Pace | Weekly Commitment | Total Duration |
|------|-------------------|----------------|
| Relaxed | 3–5 hours/week | 25–30 weeks |
| Steady | 5–7 hours/week | 17–20 weeks |
| Intensive | 10–15 hours/week | 8–12 weeks |

Adjust these estimates based on your schedule and prior experience. There is no wrong pace — consistency matters far more than speed.

## Recommended Learning Paths

Not everyone has the same goals or the same amount of time. Choose the path that best fits your situation:

### 🚀 Fast Track Path
**For:** Learners with some technical background who want to reach practical skills quickly.

```
Part 1 (skim) → Part 3 → Part 4 → Part 5 → Part 6 → Bonus Projects
```

Skip the conceptual chapters you are already comfortable with. Focus on mathematics, core ML/DL, and Generative AI. Jump straight to projects to build practical experience.

**Estimated time:** 8–10 weeks

### 📚 Deep Dive Path
**For:** Learners who want thorough, comprehensive understanding of every topic.

```
Part 1 → Part 2 → Part 3 → Part 4 → Part 5 → Part 6 → Part 7 → Part 8 → Bonus
```

Read every chapter in order. Complete every exercise. Take notes. Revisit difficult sections. This is the most complete path and produces the deepest understanding.

**Estimated time:** 20–25 weeks

### 🛠️ Project-Focused Path
**For:** Learners who want to build things as quickly as possible.

```
Part 1 → Part 4 → Part 5 → Chapter 36 (Beginner Projects)
  → Part 6 → Part 7 → Chapter 37 (Intermediate Projects)
  → Part 8 → Chapter 38 (Advanced Projects)
```

Alternate between learning and building. After each technical section, immediately apply your knowledge through projects. Revisit Part 3 (Mathematics) as needed when you encounter concepts that require deeper mathematical understanding.

**Estimated time:** 15–18 weeks

### 💼 Career Transition Path
**For:** Professionals looking to move into an AI-related role.

```
Part 1 → Part 2 → Part 4 → Part 5 → Part 6 → Part 8 (Career & Portfolio)
  → Bonus Projects → Part 7
```

Prioritize understanding, core technical skills, and career preparation. Build your portfolio early. Return to system architecture and deployment once you have secured your direction.

**Estimated time:** 15–20 weeks

## Tracking Your Progress

As you work through the book, use this simple checklist approach to track your advancement:

- ⬜ **Not started** — You have not yet read this part.
- 🔄 **In progress** — You are currently working through this part.
- ✅ **Completed** — You have finished reading, teaching, and practicing.

Mark your progress as you go. Celebrate each completed part — every stage you finish is a meaningful achievement on your journey from zero to mastery.
